Core Employee / Co-Founder Guidelines


SAFAR TECHLAB LLP

These guidelines define the professional standards, responsibilities, ethical conduct, confidentiality, and operational expectations applicable to all core employees and co-founders associated with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.


Every employee and co-founder is expected to maintain professionalism, protect company interests, and contribute responsibly toward the long-term growth and vision of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.

1. Professional Conduct

  • Maintain professionalism, discipline, and respectful behavior at all times.
  • Represent SAFAR TECHLAB LLP responsibly in both physical and digital environments.
  • Avoid abusive language, harassment, discrimination, or unethical conduct.

2. Work Responsibility

  • Complete assigned tasks within deadlines and maintain quality standards.
  • Follow instructions from reporting managers or authorized leadership.
  • Inform the management in advance regarding delays, leave, or operational issues.

3. Confidentiality & Data Security

Employees must not:

  • Share confidential company information with outsiders
  • Leak client data, passwords, systems, source code, or internal documents
  • Use company information for personal or competing business purposes

All company information remains the property of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.

4. Digital & Cyber Policy

  • Use company systems, email, and digital platforms responsibly.
  • Unauthorized access, hacking attempts, piracy, data theft, or misuse of systems is strictly prohibited.
  • Employees must protect login credentials and sensitive data.

5. Attendance & Work Hours

  • Employees are expected to maintain punctuality and regular attendance.
  • Repeated unexplained absence or late reporting may result in disciplinary action.
  • Remote employees must remain available during official working hours unless approved otherwise.

6. Social Media & Public Representation

  • Employees shall not post false, harmful, confidential, or reputation-damaging content related to the Company.
  • Any public communication representing SAFAR must be authorized.

7. Company Assets

Employees are responsible for:

  • Proper use and safety of laptops, devices, ID cards, software access, and company equipment
  • Returning all company assets immediately upon resignation or termination

8. Leave Policy

  • Leave requests should be informed and approved in advance whenever possible.
  • Emergency leave must be communicated immediately to the reporting authority.

9. Non-Compete & Ethics

During active employment, employees shall not:

  • Work for direct competitors without written approval
  • Misuse company resources for personal business activities
  • Solicit company clients or employees for outside work

10. Termination & Exit

The Company reserves the right to terminate employment in cases involving:

  • Fraud or financial misconduct
  • Confidentiality breach
  • Serious misconduct or indiscipline
  • Cybercrime or unauthorized access
  • Intentional reputational harm to the Company

11. Priority SAFAR Values

Every core employee of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP is expected to uphold:

  • Integrity
  • Innovation
  • Responsibility
  • Respect
  • Growth mindset
  • Long-term vision
