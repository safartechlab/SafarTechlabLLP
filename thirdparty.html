Third Party Association Guidelines


SAFAR TECHLAB LLP

These Third Party Association Guidelines define the professional, operational, confidentiality, and security expectations applicable to all third parties associated with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.


By engaging with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP, accessing company systems, handling company information, or providing services to the Company, you acknowledge and agree to comply with these guidelines.

1. Purpose of Association

These guidelines apply to all third parties associated with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP including:

  • Vendors
  • Agencies
  • Service providers
  • Contractors
  • External partners
  • Technical support providers
  • Marketing or operational associates

2. Professional Conduct

All third parties are expected to:

  • Maintain professional and ethical conduct
  • Communicate respectfully with company representatives, clients, and staff
  • Operate in compliance with applicable laws and regulations

3. Confidentiality

Third parties shall not disclose, copy, publish, leak, or misuse:

  • Client information
  • Internal business operations
  • Financial information
  • Technical systems or credentials
  • Project files, documents, or communication
  • Company strategies or confidential discussions

Confidentiality obligations continue even after completion of services or termination of association.

4. Data & Digital Security

Third parties must:

  • Protect all company credentials and digital access
  • Avoid unauthorized sharing or storage of sensitive data
  • Use company systems only for approved purposes
  • Immediately report any suspected security issue or data breach

5. Limited Authority

Third parties:

  • Shall not represent themselves as owners, employees, or legal representatives of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP unless officially authorized in writing
  • Shall not make commitments, financial decisions, or public statements on behalf of the Company without approval

6. Intellectual Property

Any work, designs, software, content, branding, reports, documents, or deliverables created specifically for SAFAR TECHLAB LLP during the engagement shall remain the property of the Company unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. Payment & Commercial Terms

  • Payments shall be processed as per agreed quotations, invoices, work orders, retainers, or contracts.
  • Third parties are responsible for their own taxes, registrations, licenses, and legal compliances unless otherwise agreed.

8. Non-Disclosure & Non-Misuse

Third parties shall not:

  • Use company information for personal gain or competing business activities
  • Share confidential materials with unauthorized individuals or organizations
  • Attempt unauthorized access to systems, records, or infrastructure

9. Termination of Association

SAFAR TECHLAB LLP reserves the right to immediately terminate association in cases involving:

  • Misconduct
  • Fraud or financial irregularities
  • Confidentiality breach
  • Cyber misuse or unauthorized access
  • Reputational harm to the Company
  • Violation of agreed terms or legal obligations

10. Return or Deletion of Data

Upon completion or termination of association, all confidential files, access credentials, records, devices, and company materials must be returned or permanently deleted as instructed by the Company.
