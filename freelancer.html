Freelancer Guidelines

SAFAR TECHLAB LLP

Welcome to SAFAR TECHLAB LLP (“SAFAR”, “Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). These Freelancer Guidelines define the professional expectations, responsibilities, confidentiality standards, and operational policies applicable to freelancers, consultants, independent contractors, and project-based associates working with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.


By accepting assignments, accessing company systems, or working on SAFAR projects, you acknowledge and agree to comply with these guidelines.

1. Purpose of Association

Freelancers associated with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP are engaged for temporary, project-based, creative, technical, operational, marketing, consulting, or specialized assignments.

This association does not create permanent employment unless officially confirmed in writing by the Company.

2. Professional Conduct

Freelancers are expected to:

  • Maintain professionalism and ethical behavior
  • Communicate respectfully with team members, clients, and management
  • Deliver work responsibly within agreed timelines and standards

3. Work Responsibility

  • Freelancers shall complete assigned tasks, projects, or deliverables as agreed.
  • Delays, issues, or inability to complete work must be communicated in advance.
  • Work quality and originality must be maintained at all times.

4. Confidentiality

Freelancers must not disclose, share, leak, copy, or misuse:

  • Client information
  • Company strategies
  • Internal documents
  • Source code or digital assets
  • Login credentials or access details
  • Financial or operational information

All confidential information remains property of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP even after project completion.

5. Digital & System Usage

  • Company tools, systems, and access credentials must only be used for authorized work.
  • Unauthorized access, piracy, data theft, or misuse of company systems is strictly prohibited.

6. Intellectual Property

Any work, software, content, designs, branding, graphics, reports, or deliverables created specifically for SAFAR TECHLAB LLP during the assignment shall remain property of the Company unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. Payment Terms

  • Payments shall be made according to approved project terms, milestones, invoices, or written agreements.
  • Freelancers are responsible for their own taxes and legal compliances unless otherwise specified.

8. Non-Permanent Status

Freelancers:

  • Are not permanent employees of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP
  • Are not entitled to employee benefits unless specifically approved
  • Shall not represent themselves as official employees, directors, or legal representatives of the Company without written authorization

9. Social Media & Public Representation

Freelancers shall not:

  • Share confidential project information publicly
  • Post misleading or reputation-damaging content related to the Company
  • Use Company branding without approval

10. Termination of Association

SAFAR TECHLAB LLP may terminate freelance association immediately in cases involving:

  • Misconduct
  • Confidentiality breach
  • Fraud or cyber misuse
  • Failure to deliver agreed work
  • Reputational damage to the Company

11. Return or Deletion of Data

Upon completion or termination of the assignment, freelancers must return or permanently delete all company data, credentials, documents, and project files if instructed by the Company.

12. Declaration

I confirm that:

  • I have read and understood these guidelines
  • I agree to maintain confidentiality and professionalism
  • I understand that my association with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP is project-based and non-permanent unless otherwise officially confirmed
