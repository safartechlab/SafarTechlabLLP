Fresher / Intern / Trainee Guidelines


SAFAR TECHLAB LLP

These guidelines define the professional expectations, responsibilities, learning conduct, confidentiality, and operational standards applicable to freshers, interns, and trainees associated with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.


All trainees and interns are expected to maintain professionalism, discipline, confidentiality, and active participation throughout the training or internship period.

1. Purpose of Training

Freshers, interns, and trainees are associated with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP for learning, skill development, exposure, and practical work experience.

This association does not create permanent employment unless officially confirmed by the Company in writing.

2. Professional Behavior

All trainees and interns are expected to:

  • Maintain discipline and professionalism
  • Respect team members, mentors, and workplace culture
  • Communicate respectfully in online and offline environments
  • Follow instructions provided by reporting authorities

3. Learning & Work Responsibility

  • Assigned tasks, projects, or training modules must be completed sincerely and within timelines.
  • Questions and learning participation are encouraged.
  • Repeated negligence, misconduct, or non-cooperation may result in discontinuation of the program.

4. Confidentiality

Interns, freshers, and trainees must not:

  • Share internal company information
  • Leak project files, source code, credentials, or client data
  • Record or publish confidential meetings or discussions
  • Use company data for personal or external purposes

All confidential information remains property of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.

5. Digital & System Usage

  • Company systems, tools, emails, and platforms must be used responsibly.
  • Unauthorized access, piracy, misuse of credentials, or downloading restricted data is strictly prohibited.

6. Attendance & Availability

  • Trainees and interns are expected to maintain punctuality and regular attendance.
  • Absence or leave should be informed in advance whenever possible.
  • Remote trainees must remain available during assigned hours or sessions.

7. Certificate & Completion

Internship/training certificates or recommendation letters may be issued based on:

  • Performance
  • Discipline
  • Completion of assigned work
  • Attendance and conduct

8. Stipend (If Applicable)

Any stipend, allowance, or reimbursement shall be provided only as per approved company policy or written agreement.

9. Non-Permanent Status

Freshers, interns, and trainees:

  • Are not permanent employees of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP
  • Are not entitled to employee benefits unless specifically approved
  • Cannot officially represent themselves as permanent staff or management members of the Company

10. Termination of Training

The Company reserves the right to discontinue internship or training immediately in cases involving:

  • Misconduct
  • Confidentiality breach
  • Harassment
  • Fraud or cyber misuse
  • Indiscipline or repeated negligence

11. Return of Company Materials

Any company assets, access credentials, documents, or devices provided during the internship/training must be returned upon completion or discontinuation.
