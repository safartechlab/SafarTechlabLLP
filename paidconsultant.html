Paid Professional Consultant Guidelines


SAFAR TECHLAB LLP

These guidelines define the professional, operational, confidentiality, ethical, and security expectations applicable to consultants associated with SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.


All consultants are expected to maintain professionalism, integrity, confidentiality, and responsible conduct throughout the engagement period.

1. Professional Engagement

  • Consultants are engaged for professional advisory, technical, strategic, creative, operational, or specialized services.
  • Consultants shall maintain professionalism, integrity, and ethical conduct throughout the engagement period.

2. Scope of Work

  • Consultants shall perform duties only within the agreed scope of work or assignment.
  • Any additional work outside the agreed scope must be approved by SAFAR TECHLAB LLP.

3. Confidentiality

Consultants must not disclose, share, copy, publish, or misuse:

  • Client information
  • Internal business operations
  • Financial data
  • Company strategies
  • Source code, systems, credentials, or documentation
  • Any confidential discussions or project materials

Confidentiality obligations continue even after completion or termination of the engagement.

4. Professional Conduct

Consultants are expected to:

  • Communicate respectfully and professionally
  • Maintain transparency in work and reporting
  • Avoid conflicts of interest
  • Act in the best interest of project objectives and company reputation

5. Payment Terms

  • Payments shall be made as per agreed consultancy terms, invoices, milestones, retainers, or contracts.
  • Consultants are responsible for their own taxes, registrations, and legal compliances unless otherwise specified.

6. Intellectual Property

Any reports, designs, software, documents, branding, strategies, concepts, or deliverables created specifically for SAFAR TECHLAB LLP during the engagement shall remain property of the Company unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. Non-Disclosure of Access

Consultants shall not:

  • Share company credentials or access with unauthorized persons
  • Store sensitive data insecurely
  • Use company systems beyond authorized purposes

8. Non-Representation

Consultants shall not represent themselves as:

  • Legal owners of the Company
  • Permanent employees of SAFAR TECHLAB LLP
  • Authorized spokespersons without written approval

9. Digital & Cyber Compliance

Unauthorized access, hacking, piracy, data extraction, surveillance, or misuse of digital systems is strictly prohibited and may result in legal action under applicable laws.

10. Termination of Engagement

SAFAR TECHLAB LLP may terminate the consultancy engagement immediately in cases involving:

  • Misconduct
  • Fraud
  • Confidentiality breach
  • Non-performance
  • Reputational harm
  • Violation of legal or ethical standards

11. Return of Materials

Upon completion or termination of engagement, consultants must return or permanently delete all confidential company data, documents, files, devices, and access credentials as instructed by the Company.
